How it was built

The same process I follow in a pilot with client data.

Data21 sensors and 3 operating settings per cycle. Sensors with no variation are dropped.
FeaturesRolling means and spread (5, 15 and 30 cycles) and the trend of each sensor.
ModelGradient boosting (LightGBM) with an RUL target capped at 125 cycles. Validation splits by engine, never by row.
DecisionAlert threshold, hit matrix and cost simulation to talk in euros, not RMSE.
